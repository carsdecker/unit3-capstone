Analysis (calculated from the tables above):
- retrieval_answer used the most tokens (14,362 of 24,082 = 60%) and the most cost (49%). Almost all
  of it is INPUT (13,489): every retrieval call sends the top 5 of the index's 8 chunks (~1,900–2,500
  tokens), most of them unrelated to the question.
- synthesis is 31% of cost but only 17% of tokens: it produced 1,791 of the 3,047 output tokens (59%),
  and output costs 5x input. It also runs on single-tool queries, where it only reformats a finished
  answer: synthesis on the 7 single-tool queries (1,2,3,4,8,9,10) cost $0.020154 = 18.5% of the total.
- classification is 10% (10 calls x ~330 input tokens of registry descriptions; ~4 output each).
- Query cost by route: sql $0.0036–0.0046, retrieval $0.0108–0.0160, both $0.0180–0.0224.
  The blocked unsafe query was the cheapest ($0.001866): 2 calls, then stopped.
- Cost-reduction candidates (not implemented): fewer retrieved chunks (top_k), skipping the synthesis
  call when only one agent ran, shorter synthesis output.
